African Hair

when looking at different hair types one can notice a drastic change in style and size, espicially among ethnic groups from Africa. unlike european hair, which is straight, long and shiny, African hair is described as coily, curly, or even tangled. get the refrence?

anyways african hair differs in a lot of things not just in apperance. whilst they may all look simmilar curl patterns may differ. like check out the image below.

just look at how many hair types there are and whilst you may not think about it, different hair types actually have different features. straight hair gets oily fast, it grows downward towards the shoulders, its very uniform, and slick. on the other hand take 4c for example it grows straight up, whilst the scalp still produces oil it produces much less and even less gets onto the actual hair. it coils alot resulting in it creating knots and ties within the hair.this simple detail about oil completely changes how we care for this hair type. Without oil it becomes lot drier and brittle meanning it requires different things than straight hair. instead of washing and shampooing every day like you would with the former, that type of treatment would actually kill the hair and results in breakage.instead, once a week washing and for some people even 2 weeks till washing is ideal.It all depends on climate, physical activity, and hair type. Now it goes without saying that in between these washes other things are done. things like leave in shampoos, oils and other products are applied every 3 days or so to keep in moisture, these hair type loses the abillity to really keep moisture in so products* should reflect the spefic needs for your hair type. One way to do that is by finding the porosity of your hair type and while im not going to explain it here feel free to check the link up above to find out.
*please note when buying products its best to buy from black owned buisnesses and buying specifically for black hair textures.
